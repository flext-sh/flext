## Fixed Make contract

Each public verb performs its fixed operation without an effect token, mode
selector, or validation of retired parameters. Remove the former `APPLY`
mechanism, derived check-mode branches, schemas, templates, consumers, tests,
and operational instructions. Do not replace it with another toggle.

`make docs` collects and updates documentation; `make gen` generates;
verification verbs retain their own validation responsibilities. Preserve
content, environment, and integrity validation. Change sources and regenerate
projections instead of editing generated Makefiles.

records, current integrated code, measured behavior, documentation, ADRs,
commits, PRs, and Beads. Classify each orientation as implemented, partial,
pending, superseded, or unproven, with evidence and the successor where relevant.

scope, references, and incorrect closures. Examine unmatched Beads without
inventing relationships. Apply documentation and governance changes at their
canonical owners, validate, review, integrate, and record post-merge proof before

established by history. Incorporate unique content and newer approved decisions

source ADR, plan, and Beads. Replace duplicate passages in newer ADRs with

successor and disposition in Beads so collection cannot recreate that revision.
Do not delete complete sessions or rewrite Git history.
