# FLEXT governance

## Authority

FLEXT's rules are the root [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) section "FLEXT core". Each
technical decision is an accepted record in the [ADR index](architecture/adr/README.md),
the operator's words are tracker memories cited by their key, and execution lives in
Beads. A member's `AGENTS.md` adds only its package's facts.

## Engineering conformance review

A change is accepted when it follows the FLEXT core, its real entry point shows the
claimed behavior at the claimed revision, and its gates pass; the bead records that
evidence.
